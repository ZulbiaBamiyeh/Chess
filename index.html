How to Play

This is a chess roguelike.

You climb a branching map through three acts and try to take the last king. Early fights are small boards with a few pieces. Bosses play on a full chessboard.

Capture their king to win a fight. Lose yours — or find no way to take theirs — and the run is over. Fast wins still pay more.

Pieces always come home. Gold and the occasional drop go in the bag. Shops get stranger as you go. One legendary slot until you upgrade it. One king leads the run; others wait in the bag.

The rest is on the road.

Drag or click to move. F flip · Esc put a piece down.
