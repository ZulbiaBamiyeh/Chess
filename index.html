How to Play

  • Moving. Drag a piece, or click it and click where it goes. Legal squares light up; captures get a ring.
  • Every rule is here. Castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate and stalemate, plus draws by repetition, the fifty-move rule and insufficient material.
  • Promotion. Push a pawn to the far rank and pick what it becomes — a rook or knight is sometimes the sharper choice.
  • Take-backs. U or the Take Back button rewinds your move and the reply, so you can explore a line.
  • Shortcuts. U take back · F flip the board · N new game · Esc put a piece back down.
  • Opponents. Pawn through Queen search progressively deeper. The easy ones still calculate — they just look away sometimes.
