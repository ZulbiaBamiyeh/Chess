How to Play

  • A run. A string of fights on shrinking and growing boards. Capture their king to win the fight. They win by taking yours.
  • Loadout. Lost pieces come home. Before each fight you pick what to deploy, up to that opponent's supply. Small fights really are three pieces on a tight board.
  • Army HP. The bar is the cost of whatever you still have standing. Win with more of your army intact and you leave with more gold.
  • The shop. Spend gold on pieces, a deeper reserve (+1 supply every fight), or a king passive. The bag is slot-limited by rarity.
  • Terrain. Blocked squares cannot be entered. Frost freezes a piece for a turn. A fort grants a shield — the first hit knocks the piece aside instead of taking it.
  • Moving. Drag a piece, or click it and click where it goes. U take back (classic) · F flip · Esc put a piece down.
  • Classic chess is still here — every rule, five opponents, the original board.
